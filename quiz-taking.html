Takign a Quiz

We use Canvas for in-class quizzes. When taking a quiz you will use the Respondus Lockdown Browser. You may want to watch this video for a quick overview of how the LockDown Browser works.

Preparation

Before taking your first quiz please follow the procdeure below to install the software and take a practice quiz. This will familiarize you with the process and ensure that your machine is properly set up for the quiz. You can take the practice quiz as many times as you like.
  1. Select "Quizzes" from the Canvas menu.
  2. Click on "Practice Quiz - Requires Respondus LockDown Browser". If this is the first time you're using Lockdown browser you will be prompted to download and install the software.
  3. Once the browser loads you will see the quiz start page. Note: While the browser is loading you may be prompted to grant it access to ceratin folder (e.g., "Downlaods") and to quit any other programs that interfere with its behavior (including most password managers and plug-ins). You must do this in order to continue.
  4. Click the "Begin" button. You will be prompted to enter a start code.
  5. Enter "sample-quiz" to start the quiz.
  6. To submit your answers click the "Submit" button. Note: You cannot reload the quiz after submitting. If time runs out before you submit the answers you have already selected will be automatically submitted.

After submitting you will see your answers alongside the correct ones. Please ignore any point scores as they are not automatically calculated. We will also discuss the quiz during class.

Taking a Quiz

At class time, join the Zoom meeting as you normally do. I will provide the quiz start code and answer any questions you may have before starting the quiz. Note: The Zoom meeting window will no longer appear once you start Lockdown Browser. However, Zoom will continue to run in the background and I will still see and hear you throughout the quiz.

IMPORTANT

You may not communicate with anyone or use any other device during the quiz. Your camera must remain on, and your microphone must be unmuted, throughout the quiz. If you go off camera or mute for any reason you will get a zero grade.


To take the quiz:

  1. Log into Canvas with any browser on your laptop/PC. Do not use a mobile device or tablet.
  2. Navigate to the quiz.
  3. Click to start and follow the procedure as you did above for the practice quiz.

    4. When taking a quiz, please follow these guidelines:

    • Select a quiet location where you won't be interrupted.
    • Place your computer on a flat surface such as a table or desk (not on your lap).
    • Turn off all mobile devices, phones, etc. and don't have them within reach.
    • Remain at your desk or table for the duration of the quiz.
    • Position yourself so you will be visible by the webcam during the quiz.
    • Be as quiet as possible so you don't bother other students.
    • The LockDown Browser will prevent you from accessing other websites or applications; you will be unable to exit the quiz until all questions are completed and submitted. Please do not close down the browser before submitting the quiz as this will result in a zero grade.
